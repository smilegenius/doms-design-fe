⚠️ Watch out for duplicates
Scanner portal + Smile Genius = one case, not two
Before you start, check the Smile Genius case reference against your scanner portal and existing orders. Make it once, invoice it once.
1
 When a case arrives
•Impressions come with a Smile Genius case card: your cue to open the case in Smile Genius.
•Scans are the same case you see in your scanner portal.
•Accept only once you have everything. Missing something? Comment or call first; after you accept, the dentist can only comment.
•High-volume tip: rename the dentist in your lab system to “Dr Name (Smile Genius)” so every case from them, scanned or impression, flags itself.
2
 While it’s in the lab
•Print the Smile Genius order form and keep it with the work: the reminder that it’s a Smile Genius case.
•Quote the case reference in your lab system and on your invoice.
3
 Before it ships
•Mark it Shipped before it leaves.
•New delivery date? Change it in the case and the practice is notified.
