Good to know

  • Prices are in pounds sterling, charged monthly as a subscription.
  • Your monthly allowance is the 350 cases included in your plan plus any case block you add.
  • Usage up to 5% above your allowance is a goodwill allowance and costs nothing. Cases beyond that are charged at £0.85 each.
  • Each block is its own monthly subscription. You can move to a larger block as your volume grows.
  • Integrations, such as scanner and CAD connections, are priced for each lab. Ask us for a quote.
  • Labs above 15,000 cases a month get custom pricing.
